# The Serpent Altar Crypt

## Page 1
### Entry Hall (Room 1)
You step down damp stone stairs into a chilly entry hall filled with flickering torches. Moss-covered relief carvings of coiled serpents line the walls.
Exits lead east into the Guard Post.

### Guard Post (Room 2)
An ancient armory and guard station. Rusty halberds lie scattered across cobbles.
An iron key rests inside a broken wooden chest beside an old skeleton.
Exits lead west back to the Entry Hall and north into the Serpent Corridor.

## Page 2
### Serpent Corridor (Room 3)
A long 30ft hallway decorated with brass snake statues whose eyes glitter with dark emerald glass.
A perception check (DC 12) reveals a faint seam in the east wall: a hidden secret door leading into the Secret Treasury!
Exits lead south to the Guard Post and north to the Trap Room.

### Secret Treasury (Room 4)
A small dusty chamber behind the hidden wall. Contains a stone coffer with 150 ancient silver coins and a Ring of Protection.

### Trap Room (Room 5)
A wide chamber with a suspicious pressure plate set into the flagstones.
Exits lead south to the Serpent Corridor and east to the Altar Chamber behind a heavy locked iron door.

## Page 3
### Altar Chamber (Room 6)
A dark vaulted cathedral centered around a massive Bronze Serpent Altar.
A giant viper guards the sacred altar.
The locked iron door requires the Iron Key found in the Guard Post.
Exits lead west to the Trap Room and north to the High Priest Sanctum.

### High Priest Sanctum (Room 7)
The private study of the ancient serpent cult leader.
Contains an ornate lectern with a spellbook and a golden sun talisman.
Exits lead south to the Altar Chamber and east down stairs to the Catacombs.

## Page 4
### Catacombs (Room 8)
Niches in the stone walls hold ancient mummified remains.
Exits lead west up to the Sanctum and north to the Flooded Vault.

### Flooded Vault (Room 9)
An underground spring has flooded this low room with knee-deep black water.
Exits lead south to the Catacombs and east to the Escape Tunnel.

### Escape Tunnel (Room 10)
A rough cavern passage sloping upward toward daylight and freedom! Completes the quest: "Escape the Serpent Crypt".
